About the Platform

The Agentic Research Platform uses a pipeline of 5 specialized AI agents that work together to conduct thorough academic research. Unlike simple chatbots, our agents search the web, read papers, extract findings, cross-reference facts, and synthesize everything into a structured report with citations.
